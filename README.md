# EHI

**A screen-aware AI voice assistant for Windows.** Hold a key, ask about anything on your screen, and EHI answers out loud, points at things, draws on screen, takes notes, and can do things on your PC. It always asks before it touches anything.

**[⬇ Download EHI for Windows](https://github.com/everyAi007/ehi/releases/latest)**: one file, double-click to install. Updates arrive on their own.

---

## What it does

- **Sees what you see.** Ask "what does this error mean?" or "where's the export button?" and EHI looks at your screen and answers.
- **Talks like a person.** A natural voice that runs on your PC, changes tone with its mood, and starts speaking almost instantly.
- **Lives beside your cursor.** A small glowing companion rides next to your mouse and flies over to point at whatever it's talking about. Press **Ctrl+Alt+F** to park it.
- **Works in the background.** Say "research the best budget laptops" and keep working. A helper searches the web, writes a short report and saves it to your notes.
- **Does things for you, safely.** It can open apps and websites, type, click, manage files and change settings. You choose how much it may do without asking. Anything that can't be undone always asks first, **Esc** stops it instantly, and every action is logged.
- **Takes notes and remembers.** Plain Markdown notes and lists (Obsidian-friendly), plus facts you ask it to remember, all stored on your PC.

## Your choice of brain

EHI works with the AI you prefer:

- **On your PC, free and private.** EHI can download its own brain and run fully offline. Your screen never leaves your computer.
- **Cloud AI.** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral and more, with your own key.
- **Local servers.** Ollama, LM Studio, or any Hugging Face GGUF model.

## Install

1. Download **EHI-Setup** from the [latest release](https://github.com/everyAi007/ehi/releases/latest).
2. Double-click it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because EHI is new and not yet signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Follow the short welcome tour, then hold **Right Ctrl** (you can change it) and ask something.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a microphone. The first start downloads the speech files (about 500 MB, one time).

## Shortcuts

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Hold **Right Ctrl** | Talk (let go to send) |
| Tap **Right Ctrl** twice | Type a question |
| **Ctrl+Alt+F** | Cursor buddy on / off |
| **Ctrl+Alt+D** | Draw on the screen |
| **Esc** | Stop everything |

Handy things to say: "show me how to…", "research…", "take a note…", "add milk to my shopping list", "stop following", "check for updates", "quit yourself".

## Privacy

- EHI only looks at your screen when you ask it something.
- With the on-PC brain, nothing leaves your computer.
- API keys stay in a local file on your PC and are never uploaded.
- EHI never runs as administrator.

## About the name

*Ehi* (said "eh-hee") is the Edo word for a person's guardian spirit: the little helper that watches over you.
