# Sabi

**A free, screen-aware helper for Windows, made for kids, grandparents and everyone in between.**
Hold a key and ask about anything on your screen. Sabi answers out loud and draws on your screen like a friend
with a marker, and its drawings come alive to show you how things work.

**[⬇ Download Sabi for Windows](https://github.com/everyAi007/ehi/releases/latest)**: one file, double-click to install. Updates arrive on their own.

*Sabi was called EHI before version 1.6. If you have EHI, it will offer the update by itself.*

## What it does
- **Sees what you see.** Ask "where's the attach button?" or "what does this error mean?" and Sabi looks at your screen, answers, and points at the right spot.
- **Drawings that come alive.** While it explains, objects bob, float, orbit, rain and talk: clouds rain, the moon circles the earth, data flies from the router to your laptop.
- **Picture-book lessons.** 13 short lessons with a quiz at the end: rain, Wi-Fi, the sun and moon, plants, email, gravity, bees, day and night, butterflies, rainbows, staying safe online and more. Say "what can you teach me?"
- **Walks you through things.** Say "show me how to…" and Sabi shows one step at a time and waits for you.
- **Easy to read.** Say "make the text bigger" any time.
- **Does things for you, safely.** It can open apps and websites, type and click. You choose how much it may do without asking, anything that can't be undone always asks first, and Esc stops it instantly.

## Your choice of brain
- **On your PC, free and private.** Sabi can download its own AI once and then run fully offline. Your screen never leaves your computer.
- **Cloud AI.** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq and more, with your own key.
- **Local servers.** Ollama, LM Studio, or any Hugging Face GGUF model.

## Install
1. Download **Sabi-Setup** from the [latest release](https://github.com/everyAi007/ehi/releases/latest).
2. Double-click it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because Sabi is new and not yet signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the short welcome, then hold **Right Ctrl** (you can change it) and ask something.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a microphone.

## Shortcuts
| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Hold **Right Ctrl** | Talk (let go to send) |
| Tap **Right Ctrl** twice, or **Ctrl+Alt+T** | Type a question |
| **Ctrl+Alt+C** | Circle something, then ask about it |
| **Ctrl+Alt+P** | Pause Sabi (not looking, not listening) |
| **Esc** | Stop everything |

## Privacy
- Sabi only looks at your screen when you ask it something.
- With the on-PC brain, nothing leaves your computer.
- API keys stay on your PC, locked with Windows so only your account can read them.
- Sabi never runs as administrator.

## Feedback
Say "send feedback" or use the tray menu. Sabi makes a zip with its logs, with your conversations, keys and name removed, and nothing is sent until you send it. You can also [open an issue](https://github.com/everyAi007/ehi/issues/new).
